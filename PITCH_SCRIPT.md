# Pitch Script (2–3 minutes, ~340 words)

Screen-record the live site on desktop, then flip to mobile view near the end. Your pitch goes to the owner, so speak to him directly.

**[0:00 – Show hero]**
Hello, I'm Gurjot. I've built a website for Prime Fresh Oil Mills. Before designing anything I asked: who visits this site? Not households. It's edible oil brands, FMCG companies and private-label businesses looking for a supplier. They want to know what you supply, whether they can trust you, and how to get a price.

**[0:25 – Point at header, hero buttons]**
So the site is built around one action: Request Quote. It's in the header, the hero, on every product, and on mobile it stays at the bottom with Call and WhatsApp beside it.

**[0:45 – Hero product section]**
Yellow Mustard Oil, your bestselling oil, has its own section with a direct quote button.

**[1:05 – Catalog, click "Add to quote" on 2–3 products]**
Your catalog covers oils, seeds and de-oiled cakes. A brand or FMCG buyer rarely wants just one item, so every product has "Add to quote". Watch the counter follow me as I add Yellow Mustard Oil, groundnut seeds and a mustard cake.

**[1:30 – Quote form]**
Now the form shows one line per product, each with its own quantity, unit and packing. One request, one consolidated quotation. The form also asks business type, delivery city, whether it's for their own brand, and samples. I submit, and the buyer gets a clear summary, and can send the same list to you on WhatsApp in one tap, because that's how most Indian B2B deals actually start.

**[1:55 – Facility videos]**
Buyers want to see the place before they commit, so I used your own facility videos, the processing equipment and the warehouse, instead of stock photos.

**[2:15 – FAQ, For brands]**
There's a simple three-step enquiry flow for brand and private-label buyers, and an FAQ that answers the first questions they'd otherwise call to ask.

**[2:30 – Mobile view, contact]**
It works the same on a phone, with your number and email one tap away.

**[2:45 – Close]**
I only used facts I could confirm. Prices, pack sizes and certifications go into the quotation, so every enquiry reaches you ready to quote. Thank you.
