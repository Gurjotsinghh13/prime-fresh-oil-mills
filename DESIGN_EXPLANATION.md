# Design Explanation – Prime Fresh Oil Mills

## Who the site is for
Not shoppers. The visitors are **procurement and sourcing people at edible oil brands, FMCG companies and private-label businesses**. They compare several suppliers, often on a phone, and need three answers fast: *What do you supply? Can I trust you? How do I get a price?*

## Primary action: Request Quote
The whole page is organised around one conversion. "Request Quote" is in the header, the hero, the hero-product section, the contact block, and a sticky bar on mobile (Call / Request Quote / WhatsApp). Every catalog card has "Add to quote", which builds a list, and a floating counter keeps it visible while browsing, so a buyer can collect everything they need before filling in the form once.

## Page flow (follows the buyer's decision path)
1. **Hero:** who we serve and what we supply, with Yellow Mustard Oil as the headline product.
2. **Hero product section:** Yellow Mustard Oil on its own, with a direct quote button and a WhatsApp option.
3. **Catalog:** oils, seeds and de-oiled cakes with category filters. Bestsellers are flagged so buyers see what moves.
4. **Inside our facility:** the owner's own facility videos. A buyer sourcing from a manufacturer wants to see the place.
5. **For brands:** a plain 3-step enquiry process for private-label buyers.
6. **Multi-product quote list and form:** buyers add several oils, seeds and cakes with "Add to quote", set quantity, unit and packing per product, and send one consolidated request.
7. **FAQ and contact:** phone, email and WhatsApp always one tap away.

## Visual identity
- **Colour** comes straight from the client's logo: mustard gold and deep leaf green on warm cream. Gold is reserved for actions, so the eye lands on Request Quote.
- **Type:** Fraunces (warm serif) for headlines gives heritage; Inter keeps forms and details readable.
- **Imagery:** the owner's real photographs, cropped consistently. Unedited facility video instead of stock imagery, because authenticity builds trust with buyers.
- **Restraint:** no carousels or heavy animation.

## Honesty as a design decision
The brief supplied no prices, specs, certifications or pack sizes, and no photo of the oil itself. I chose not to invent them. Oil cards use a clearly labelled illustration rather than a seed photo posing as oil, and any detail the owner hasn't confirmed is routed into the quotation ("price, packing and MOQ shared in your quotation"). A buyer who spots a made-up claim stops trusting the supplier.

## Mobile
Single-column layout, 44px+ tap targets, correct keyboards on form fields (tel, email, numeric), sticky bottom action bar, and videos that load only when played.

## What was missing from the brief (bonus)
| Gap | What I added |
|---|---|
| The brief shows one product; the business sells three product families, and its buyers (FMCG, oil brands, private label) typically source several items together | Full catalog plus a **multi-product quote list**: select any mix of oils, seeds and cakes, set quantity, unit and packing for each, and send one consolidated request |
| Buyers want proof of a real operation | Facility videos section |
| B2B enquiries need more than name and email | Business type, order type (monthly / one-time), per-product volume and packing, delivery city, own-brand and sample fields |
| Indian B2B deals run on WhatsApp | WhatsApp buttons and a "Send on WhatsApp" that sends the whole product list as one numbered message |
| Brand buyers need to know how to start | "For brands" 3-step enquiry flow |
| Unanswered objections | Buyer FAQ |
| Findability | SEO title/description, structured data, accessible markup, keyboard focus styles, reduced-motion support |

## Product thinking
Every section removes a reason for a buyer to leave and call. For the owner, each enquiry arrives with product, volume, packing, location and brand intent already captured, so the first sales call can start at price.
