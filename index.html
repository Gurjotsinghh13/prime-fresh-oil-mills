Buyer FAQ

Procurement questions buyers ask first

Clear answers to commercial, quality, and supply questions for purchasing managers.

What product categories and items do you supply?

We supply three mill-direct product categories: Edible Oils (Yellow Mustard Oil, Black Mustard Oil), Edible Seeds (Yellow Mustard Seeds, Black Mustard Seeds, Groundnut Seeds, White & Black Sesame Seeds), and De-oiled Cakes (Yellow Mustard Cake, Black Mustard Cake). All extracted and processed without chemical adulteration.

What commercial packaging formats are available for bulk and private label?

For oils, we provide stainless tanker loads, 200L MS/HDPE drums, 15L/15kg commercial tins, and retail packing (1L, 2L, 5L PET bottles or pouches) for private-label brands. For seeds and de-oiled cakes, we supply in 25kg and 50kg HDPE/jute bags as well as full truckloads.

What are your prices, payment terms, and Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Because agricultural commodities fluctuate with mandi benchmarks, prices and MOQs depend on current raw material rates, batch volume, and delivery destination. Submit a quotation request or WhatsApp message to receive competitive, mill-direct pricing within 24 hours.

Can our QA team receive lab-tested samples before we place an order?

Yes. Simply check the sample option in the quote form. We dispatch physical lots along with a batch Certificate of Analysis (COA) so your laboratory can verify moisture, acid value, and purity parameters prior to commercial contracting.

Do you manufacture for third-party and private-label brands?

Yes. We work directly with FMCG brands and private-label businesses, assisting with batch production, packing into brand-labelled bottles, tins, or drums, and adhering to statutory packaging requirements.

What is your typical turnaround time for quotations and dispatches?

Quotation requests are reviewed and quoted within 24 business hours. Sample dispatches typically take 3 to 5 working days. Commercial bulk dispatches are scheduled based on batch volume and transport agreements.

Where do you deliver across India?

We supply trade buyers pan-India. Specify your delivery city and state in the inquiry, and we will quote CIF (delivered) or ex-mill freight terms based on your transport preferences.

Can institutional buyers visit or audit the mill?

Yes. We welcome verified brand partners and institutional procurement teams to inspect our processing and warehousing facility by prior appointment.

What is the fastest way to connect with your sales desk?

Call or WhatsApp us directly on +91 90572 92529. Our commercial team responds promptly during trade hours.
