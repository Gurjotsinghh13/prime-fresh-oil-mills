Buyer FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

What do you supply?

Edible oils, edible seeds and de-oiled cakes. Our bestsellers are Yellow Mustard Oil, groundnut seeds, and sesame seeds and oil.

What are your prices and minimum order quantity?

They depend on the product, volume and packing. Send a quote request and our team will reply with exact terms.

Can I get a sample?

Tick the sample box in the quote form and mention your delivery address. Our team will confirm what is possible.

Can you supply for my own brand?

Select "Yes, private label" in the form and tell us your requirement. We will confirm what we can do.

Where do you deliver?

Share your delivery city in the form and we will confirm. You can also call or WhatsApp us.

What is the fastest way to reach you?

Call or WhatsApp us on the number in the header and contact section.
